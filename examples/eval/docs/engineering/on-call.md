# On-call

Each platform team runs a weekly on-call rotation. The rotation hands over on Monday at 10:00,
with a short call between the outgoing and incoming engineer.

## Responsibilities

Pages come through PagerTide. Acknowledge a page within 15 minutes, day or night. If you cannot,
the page escalates to the secondary on-call engineer, then to the engineering manager.

## Compensation

On-call engineers receive 250 euros per week of on-call duty, plus 60 euros for each incident
they handle outside working hours. After a night with an incident, start late the next day.

## Swaps

You can swap shifts with a teammate; update the PagerTide schedule yourself and tell your team.
