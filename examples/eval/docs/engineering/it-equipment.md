# IT equipment

## Laptops

Choose a 14-inch or 16-inch laptop when you join. Laptops are replaced every four years, or
sooner if they break. Each desk in the office has a docking station and a second monitor; a
monitor for home can be ordered from IT.

## Phones

Tessaly does not issue phones by default. If you use your own phone for work, you receive a
phone allowance of 25 euros per month. Engineers on the on-call rotation get a company phone.

## Leaving Tessaly

Return all equipment within five working days of your last day. IT sends a prepaid box to
remote employees.
