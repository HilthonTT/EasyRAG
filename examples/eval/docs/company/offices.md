# Offices

## Bergen (headquarters)

Our Bergen office is in the Sandviken district, on four floors of a converted fish warehouse.
It houses management, research and development, and the production line. Parking is limited to
visitors and employees with a mobility need; everyone else is encouraged to take the bus or
use the bike storage in the basement.

## Rotterdam

The Rotterdam office opened in 2014 and handles sales and service for Europe, Africa and the
Middle East. It also runs the calibration laboratory and the European spare-parts warehouse.

## Halifax

The Halifax office opened in 2018 and is home to the North American field service team. From
June to August the Halifax office closes at 15:00 on Fridays ("summer hours"). Halifax is five
hours behind Bergen for most of the year, so meetings with all three offices are scheduled
between 14:00 and 16:00 Bergen time.

## Visiting another office

Employees can work from any office. Book a desk in the Harbor system at least one working day in
advance so reception can prepare a badge.
