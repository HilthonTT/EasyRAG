# About Tessaly Marine Instruments

Tessaly Marine Instruments builds sensors and data platforms for measuring the ocean. Ports,
offshore operators and research institutes use our equipment to track tides, water quality and
sea state.

## History

The company was founded in 2009 in Bergen, Norway, by oceanographer Ingrid Solheim and
electronics engineer Mateo Varga. The first product was a hand-built tide gauge for the Port of
Bergen. The TideWatch line followed in 2016 and is still our best-selling product family.

In 2014 we opened a sales and service office in Rotterdam, and in 2018 a field service office in
Halifax, Canada, to support customers in North America.

## Today

Tessaly employs about 340 people across three offices. Priya Raman has been chief executive
since 2021; co-founder Ingrid Solheim chairs the board. Headquarters, research and manufacturing
are in Bergen.

Our mission statement is "Measure the sea honestly": every instrument ships with its full
calibration record, and we publish the accuracy we measure, not the accuracy we hope for.
