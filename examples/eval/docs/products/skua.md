# Skua CTD probe

The Skua measures conductivity, temperature and depth (CTD) as it is lowered through the water
column. Salinity and density are calculated from those three readings.

## Specifications

- Depth rating: 2,000 metres
- Sampling rate: 16 Hz
- Housing: titanium
- Data: stored internally and downloaded over USB-C after recovery

## Calibration

The conductivity and temperature sensors drift slowly. Send each Skua for calibration every 12
months. Calibration is done at our laboratory in Rotterdam and takes about two weeks, including
shipping within Europe.
