# TideWatch tide gauges

TideWatch gauges measure water level from above the surface with a downward-looking radar, so
nothing touches the water and there is nothing to foul.

## TideWatch T3

Launched in 2016, the T3 measures water level with an accuracy of plus or minus 2 millimetres
at one reading per second. It runs on a lithium battery pack that lasts five years with the
default one-minute reporting interval, and sends data over a VHF radio link to a shore station.

## TideWatch T4

The T4, released in 2023, replaces the radio link with LTE-M cellular, so no shore station is
needed. It keeps the T3's radar and accuracy and adds a built-in air-temperature and barometric
pressure sensor. Existing T3 mounting brackets fit the T4 without changes.

## Support

The T3 will receive firmware updates until the end of 2027.
