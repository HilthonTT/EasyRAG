# Warranty and returns

## Warranty periods

Every Tessaly instrument has a standard warranty of 24 months from delivery. TideWatch gauges
have 36 months, because they are mounted above the water. Customers can buy an extended
warranty that brings the total up to five years.

## What is not covered

Damage from biofouling, collisions with vessels, and opening a sealed housing are not covered.

## Returns

Request a return merchandise authorisation (RMA) in the service portal before shipping anything.
Returns from Europe, Africa and the Middle East go to Rotterdam; returns from the Americas go to
Halifax. Repairs under warranty are free, including return shipping.
