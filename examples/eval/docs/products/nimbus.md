# Nimbus buoy platform

Nimbus is a moored data buoy that carries other instruments far from shore.

## Power and communication

Nimbus is powered by three solar panels charging a lead-carbon battery bank, enough for two
weeks without sun. It sends its data over an Iridium satellite uplink every 30 minutes, and
switches to every 5 minutes when wave height exceeds a configurable alarm level.

## Payload

The platform hosts up to six sensors, connected through sealed connectors in the instrument
well. Supported sensors include the Skua CTD probe, a wave sensor and a current profiler.

## Deployment

The buoy weighs 180 kilograms without its mooring. Deploying it needs a vessel with an A-frame
or crane rated for at least 500 kilograms.
