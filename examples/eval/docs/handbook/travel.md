# Travel policy

All business travel is booked through Voyager, our travel portal. Bookings made elsewhere are
only reimbursed if Voyager could not offer the trip.

## Getting there

Take the train for journeys under four hours. For flights, book economy class when the flight
is shorter than six hours and premium economy when it is six hours or longer. Business class is
never reimbursed.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 160 euros. In London, Oslo and Zurich the limit is 210 euros because
of local prices.

## Per diem

Instead of claiming individual meals while travelling, you receive a daily allowance: 55 euros
per day within the EU and 70 euros per day outside it. Meals paid by a client or at a
conference reduce the allowance for that day.

## Approval

Trips are approved by your manager. A trip whose total estimated cost is above 2,000 euros also
needs approval from your department head before booking.
