# Expense claims

Claim business expenses in Ledgerline, our finance system, within 30 days of the purchase.
Claims submitted later are only paid with written approval from Finance.

## Receipts

Attach a receipt for every expense of 25 euros or more. For smaller amounts a short description
is enough. A photo of the receipt is fine; you do not need to keep the paper copy.

## Mileage

Driving your own car for work is reimbursed at 0.32 euros per kilometre. Commuting to your usual
office does not count.

## Client meals

Client meals are capped at 80 euros per person, including the client. Alcohol is only
reimbursed at client dinners, and at most two drinks per person.

## Payment

Approved claims are paid into your salary account within ten working days. Your manager
approves claims; claims from managers are approved by their own manager.
