# Parental leave

Tessaly tops up statutory parental pay so that you receive your full salary during leave.

## How much leave

- Primary carer: 20 weeks at full pay.
- Secondary carer: 6 weeks at full pay.

The same rules apply to birth, adoption and surrogacy.

## Eligibility

You qualify once you have worked at Tessaly for six months. Before that, statutory parental
leave still applies, without the top-up.

## Taking the leave

Leave can be split into up to three blocks, all taken within 18 months of the birth or
placement. During leave you may work up to ten "keeping-in-touch" days, paid as normal, to join
team events or training. They are optional and do not end your leave.
