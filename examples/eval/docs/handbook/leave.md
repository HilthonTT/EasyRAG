# Annual leave and sick leave

## Annual leave

Full-time employees get 27 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees get
a pro-rata share.

Request leave in the Harbor HR system. Requests of more than three consecutive days must be made
at least two weeks in advance.

You may carry over at most five unused days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used
by 31 March or they expire.

The offices close from 24 December to 1 January. These closure days are not deducted from your
annual leave.

## Sick leave

If you are ill, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day. From the fourth consecutive
day of illness you need a doctor's note. Sick days never reduce your annual leave.
