# Learning and development

Every employee has a personal learning budget of 1,500 euros per calendar year, plus five paid
learning days.

## What it covers

Courses, conferences (ticket, travel and hotel), certifications and books. Books under 60 euros
can be bought without approval; everything else needs your manager's approval in Harbor.

Retakes of failed certification exams are not covered.

## Rules

Unused budget does not roll over to the next year. If you leave Tessaly within twelve months of
a course that cost more than 3,000 euros (paid with a special exception), you repay half of it.
