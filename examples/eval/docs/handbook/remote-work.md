# Hybrid and remote work

Tessaly is a hybrid company. Teams decide their own rhythm, with two rules: everyone spends at
least two days per week in an office, and Tuesday is the anchor day when everyone in your team
comes in.

## Working from abroad

You may work remotely from another country for up to four weeks per calendar year, as long as
the country is in the EU or EEA. Your manager and HR must approve it in advance, for tax and
insurance reasons.

## Home office

New employees receive a one-off home office allowance of 400 euros for furniture and a monthly
internet allowance of 30 euros. Laptops and monitors are provided by IT, see the IT equipment
page.
