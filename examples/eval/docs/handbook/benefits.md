# Benefits

## Pension

Tessaly matches your pension contributions up to 6% of your gross salary. If you contribute 4%,
we add 4%; if you contribute 8%, we add 6%.

## Health insurance

All employees and their families are covered by a private health insurance plan from Nordvita.
The plan includes dental care and physiotherapy.

## Staying active

- Gym subsidy: 35 euros per month towards any gym or sports club membership.
- Bike lease: lease an e-bike through salary deduction, with a lease term of three years.

## Other

Employees receive a paid day off on their birthday, and the company pays for an annual health
check from age 40.
