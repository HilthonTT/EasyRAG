# Your first weeks

## First day

Arrive at 09:30 at the reception of your office. Your manager meets you there and introduces you
to your buddy, a colleague from another team who helps you find your way during the first
months. Your laptop is already set up by IT.

## Required training

Complete the security awareness training within your first 14 days. You cannot get access to
production systems until it is done.

## Probation

The probation period is six months. You have check-ins with your manager after 30, 60 and 90
days, and a probation review at the end of month six.
